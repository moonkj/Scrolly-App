Support
Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, please reach out via the App Store developer contact page.

How to use

1. Enable Scrolly in Settings → Safari → Extensions.
2. Open Safari, tap the puzzle icon in the address bar.
3. Tap Scrolly to start auto-scrolling.

Compatibility

Requires iOS 17 or later with Safari.
