Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025  ·  Effective: March 2025

1. Information We Collect

Scrolly collects no personal information whatsoever — no names, emails, location data, device identifiers, or usage statistics.

2. Local Storage

Only your scroll settings (speed, direction, loop, timer, etc.) and widget position are stored, exclusively on your device via Safari's extension storage. This data never leaves your device and is inaccessible to anyone, including the developer.

3. Network Communication

Scrolly makes zero network requests. It works fully offline without an internet connection.

4. Third-Party Services

No advertising networks, analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics), social logins, or third-party payment SDKs are used.

5. Children's Privacy

Scrolly does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no data collection of any kind.

6. Policy Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced via app updates or this page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please use the developer contact on the App Store page.
